# Forum Instructions

Six times this semester, class becomes a book club. Each Forum session is built around one chapter of *Counting: How We Use Numbers to Decide What Matters* by Deborah Stone, connected to whatever machine learning topic we're covering that week.

This page explains how the in-class activity works and how to submit it. The discussion questions themselves are different every time — find them on that week's own Forum page, linked in the table below.

## How it works

1. **You'll get a paper** with that week's discussion questions printed on it.
2. **Annotate it yourself first.** Read the questions and write your own thoughts, reactions, and answers directly on your copy — this is your own thinking, before anyone else's.
3. **Compare with classmates.** At the end of the in-class discussion, talk with other students about their answers, and add their impressions to your own paper too — in your own words, noting whose idea it was. Your final page should show both your own initial thinking and what you picked up from others.
4. **Photograph or scan it and upload it to Moodle.** A clear phone photo is fine, as long as your handwriting is legible.
