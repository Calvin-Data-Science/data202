# Forum 6 — Instructions

*Week 15 — Project*

## Reading

Read **Chapter 6: "The Ethics of Counting"** from *Counting: How We Use Numbers to Decide What Matters* by Deborah Stone.

Stone's argument: counting is, at bottom, a judgment call with ethical stakes, and numbers by themselves can't resolve that judgment — only dialogue, principles, and humility can. "Treat likes alike" sounds like a neutral fairness formula, but the real question is always *which* likeness matters. A hiring point system that rewards publishing over teaching, a recidivism algorithm that learns yesterday's bias as today's prediction, a birthday cutoff that hands some kids a permanent developmental edge — in every case, a human judgment is lurking inside the number, whether or not anyone admits it.
