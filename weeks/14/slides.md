## The Full ML Workflow

1. **Frame** the problem — who is it for? what is success?
2. **Acquire & explore** data — provenance, quality, gaps
3. **Engineer features** — choices encode values
4. **Train & evaluate** models — validation, not just accuracy
5. **Interpret & audit** — explain, measure disparities
6. **Deploy thoughtfully** — who is affected?

---

## What Makes a Responsible Practitioner?

- Technical competence is necessary but not sufficient
- Ask: *who benefits and who is harmed?*
- Include stakeholders in problem definition
- Document assumptions and limitations
- Know when **not** to build a model
